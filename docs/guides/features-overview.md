# What you can do with Crona

Crona keeps the workday connected without trying to become a cloud dashboard. Start with the part you need and follow the links when you want more detail.

## Plan the day

Use repositories, streams, and issues to turn a loose list into a day you can actually see.

[Plan issues and daily work](../workflows/issues-and-planning.md)

## Focus on an issue

Start Pomodoro or no-break timer sessions directly from the issue. Pause, resume, stash an interruption, and finish with a useful summary.

[Run focus sessions](../workflows/focus-sessions.md)

## Keep recurring work visible

Track habits alongside issues, configure their cadence, and use Momentum to see continuity without turning the day into a scorecard.

[Track habits](../workflows/habits.md)

## Notice how the workday feels

Record check-ins, look at wellbeing trends, and use protected rest or away days without losing the history around them.

[Use wellbeing and check-ins](../workflows/check-ins-and-wellbeing.md)

## Keep a useful record

Read the day in Summary, browse completed sessions, and export Markdown, CSV, PDF, or calendar files when you need an artifact outside Crona.

[Review and export your work](../workflows/exports-and-reports.md)

## Use the surface that fits

The TUI is the deepest interactive surface. The CLI is for commands and automation. The macOS Companion is for quick actions close at hand. They all use the same local daemon and state.

[Read about the macOS Companion](../workflows/macos-companion.md) · [Use the CLI](../reference/cli-and-local-engine.md)
